# UN Careers (Inspira) and agency portals

- Sign-in is the candidate's step. The personal history profile (P11) is reused across applications;
  check it against the fact base before each one.
- Most applications have a motivation or cover-note box with a character limit instead of a letter
  upload: paste the letter body, shortened to fit, without the address block.
- Competency questions: one concrete example each.
- Submission confirmation appears under "My applications" with a status.
